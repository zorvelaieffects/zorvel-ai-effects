# Zorvel: AI Cartoon Filter and Short AI Video Effects

[Zorvel](https://zorvel.app/) turns one photo into an AI cartoon filter or a short AI video effect.

This repository is an independent, factual introduction to the live product. The official product page with samples is [zorvel.app/effects](https://zorvel.app/effects).

## What you can make

Zorvel currently offers eight live effects:

- **Cartoon Filter** — a static cartoon-style image (20 credits)
- **Drawings to Life** — a short video effect
- **Orbit Pullback** — a short video effect
- **Chrome Bloom** — a short video effect
- **Paper World** — a short video effect
- **Gravity Drift** — a short video effect
- **Cloud Dive** — a short video effect
- **Studio Echo** — a short video effect

The seven video effects are about 4–5 seconds long and cost 116–200 credits each.

## Pricing

Zorvel is a paid product. There is no free tier.

- Credits cost $0.01 each
- Credit packs stay valid for 12 months
- Monthly and yearly plans are also available

See current options on the [effects page](https://zorvel.app/effects).
